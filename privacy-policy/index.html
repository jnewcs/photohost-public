Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10/31/2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. You can reach us at saumyasinghalphotography@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact and booking details you provide: name, email address, phone number, event details, and any other information you share when inquiring or booking.
  • Photographs and media created during sessions, including edited images delivered through private online galleries.
  • Gallery activity: when a gallery is opened, which photos are viewed or downloaded, download request details, and technical information such as IP address, browser, and timestamps.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, text, or through this website, and records of messages we send you, including delivery status.
  • Payment information, where applicable, processed by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.

2. How we use your information

  • We use personal information to provide and deliver photography services; create and share private galleries; send transactional emails and text messages about your gallery, downloads, and appointments; respond to inquiries; maintain records of consent and communications; secure our services; and comply with legal obligations.
  • We do not use your mobile number or SMS consent for marketing unless you separately opt in to marketing messages.

3. Text messaging (SMS) program

  • We may send text messages to the mobile number you provide in connection with our services, such as notifying you that your photos are ready or that a gallery is available. Marketing and transactional text messages are offered through separate, voluntary opt-ins; consent to SMS is never a condition of purchasing our services, and SMS consent is separate from consent to receive email.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. For assistance, contact saumyasinghalphotography@gmail.com. Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages. You can withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP.
  • Text messaging opt-in data and consent: All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

4. How we share information

  • We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate our business, such as website hosting, cloud photo storage, and email/SMS delivery providers; when required by law or to protect our rights; and with your direction or consent. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share mobile opt-in data or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.
  • Our current providers include: GitHub Pages (website hosting), Cloudflare Turnstile (contact form bot protection), Amazon Web Services (photo storage), Brevo (email and SMS delivery), and Stripe (payments). Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy terms.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember preferences, and understand aggregate usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functionality. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of you.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain business and consent records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. When no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it. Gallery access links may expire, and galleries may be removed after the delivery period described in your booking agreement.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including private galleries, access controls, encrypted connections, and restricted access to client data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw SMS or email consent at any time. Contact us at saumyasinghalphotography@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will reflect the latest version, and material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
